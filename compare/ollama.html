QUIET Ai vs Ollama

Ollama runs open AI models on a Mac, Linux or Windows computer, or in Ollama's cloud. QUIET Ai runs them on an iPhone or iPad. They are made for different devices, and many people use both.

Pick Ollama if

You want models on a computer, you are building an app or agent that needs a local API, or you need models far bigger than a phone can hold.

Pick QUIET Ai if

You want a private AI in your pocket that works on a plane or with no signal, with no setup and no command line.

Questions

Is there an Ollama app for iPhone?

Ollama's download page lists macOS, Linux and Windows. To run a model on the iPhone itself you need an iPhone app such as QUIET Ai.

Can I use Ollama and QUIET Ai together?

Yes. Use Ollama on your computer for large models, and QUIET Ai on your iPhone or iPad for private chat away from your desk. They do not share models or chats today.

Do they run the same models?

Mostly the same families: Gemma, Qwen, Llama, DeepSeek, Mistral and Phi. A phone holds smaller versions, usually 1B to 8B.
