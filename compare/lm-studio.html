QUIET Ai vs LM Studio

LM Studio is a desktop app for running open AI models on Mac, Linux and Windows. Its makers also publish an iPhone app called Locally AI. QUIET Ai is built only for iPhone and iPad, around one question: what will this device run well?

Pick LM Studio if

You work at a computer and want a full desktop app with a local server, or you want to reach a large model on your own machine from another device.

Pick QUIET Ai if

You want to bring your own GGUF models to an iPhone, including uncensored ones, and see exactly what fits before downloading several gigabytes.

Questions

Is LM Studio available on iPhone?

LM Studio itself is a desktop app. Its makers publish Locally AI by LM Studio for iPhone and iPad.

What is the difference between QUIET Ai and Locally AI?

Both run models on the iPhone without an account. QUIET Ai uses GGUF files, so you can add any public GGUF from Hugging Face, and it rates every model against your device's memory before you download.

Can QUIET Ai connect to LM Studio on my Mac?

Not today. QUIET Ai runs every model on the iPhone or iPad itself.
