Questions

What is an uncensored AI model?

An open model that has been tuned, or edited, to follow your instructions instead of refusing. Dolphin and Hermes are trained that way; abliterated models have the refusal behaviour removed from an existing model such as Llama, Qwen or Gemma.

Can I run an uncensored AI on my iPhone?

Yes. QUIET Ai runs these models on the iPhone or iPad itself. Models of 3B and smaller run on most recent iPhones; 7B and 8B models want an 8 GB or 12 GB iPhone (iPhone 15 Pro and later). QUIET Ai checks your device before downloading.

Is it private?

The model runs on your device and works in Airplane Mode. Your chats are not sent to a server, there is no account, and nobody reviews what you type.

How do I add one to QUIET Ai?

Open Models, then More, then Add from Hugging Face. Paste the repository name shown on the model's page and pick the Q4_K_M file.

Is it allowed?

These are openly published models with their own licences, linked on each page. QUIET Ai is rated 18+. You are responsible for what you make with them and for following the law where you live.
