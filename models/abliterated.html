Questions

What does abliterated mean?

Researchers found that a model's refusals mostly run through one direction inside its network. Abliteration finds that direction and removes it from the weights. The model keeps what it knows, and stops answering with a refusal.

How is abliterated different from uncensored?

Uncensored is the general word. Abliterated names one method: editing the weights of an existing model. Dolphin and Hermes reach a similar result by training on data without refusals.

Does abliteration make a model worse?

Sometimes slightly: answers can be a little less careful than the original. Newer versions (often marked v2) are close to the original model in quality.

Which abliterated model should I try first?

On an 8 GB iPhone, Qwen3 4B Abliterated or Gemma 3 4B Abliterated. On a 12 GB iPhone or an iPad, Llama 3.1 8B Instruct Abliterated or Qwen3 8B Abliterated.
