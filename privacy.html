Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

The short version
Your chats, documents, notes and photos never leave your device. There is no account. If you choose to turn on Share anonymous usage (off by default), the app sends anonymous counts such as which features are used, plus crash reports — never anything you write.

This policy explains how QUIET Ai ("the app", "we"), developed by Essential Dev Team, handles information. Our App Store privacy label lists Usage Data and Diagnostics, not linked to you. These are collected only if you turn on Share anonymous usage, starting with version 1.3. With it off — the default — nothing is collected.

What the app does with your information

Everything happens on your device:

  • Your messages and the AI's replies are processed by a language model running on your iPhone's processor and stored in the app's private storage on the phone. They are never transmitted to us or to anyone else.
  • Photos you attach or take with the camera (for vision-capable models) are resized and stored in the app's storage on your phone only.
  • Voice conversations (from version 1.6): the microphone is used only while you talk. Your speech is turned into text on your device by Apple's on-device speech recognition. Audio is never recorded, stored or sent. iOS may download Apple's speech model the first time, from Apple.
  • Calendar and reminders: only if you allow it and type @ in a chat, the app reads your next week of events or your open reminders on your device, so the model can answer questions about them. They are not stored by the app or sent anywhere.
  • Device information (chip, memory, free storage, thermal state) is read locally to recommend models that fit. It is displayed to you and not sent anywhere.
  • Settings and preferences are stored on your phone.

The only network connections the app makes

The app routes every request of its own through a single allow-list. These are the only destinations it can reach. Apple system services you turn on, such as iCloud Drive backup and the speech model for voice, are handled by iOS under Apple's privacy policy.

Optional anonymous usage data (off by default)

From version 1.3 you can turn on Share anonymous usage in Settings › Privacy. Nothing is collected until you do, and turning it off stops collection immediately and deletes anything not yet sent.

When it is on, the app sends:

  • a random installation ID created by the app (not linked to your Apple Account, name, email or any advertising identifier; it is deleted if you delete the app);
  • device model, iOS and app version, language, region and time zone;
  • when sessions start and end, and which screen was last open;
  • counts of what was used — for example "a reply finished on a Qwen model at 20 tokens per second", "a PDF with 12 pages was added", "@calendar was used";
  • purchase events from Apple for earlier subscriptions (renewed, cancelled) without prices, receipts or transaction IDs;
  • crash and performance reports from Apple's MetricKit.

It never sends your messages, the AI's replies, document contents or names, chat titles, notes, "About you" text, photos, calendar or reminder contents, your IP address (it is not stored), location, or any advertising identifier. The data is stored with Supabase on our own project, used only to improve the app, never sold or shared, and deleted after 13 months. To have it deleted sooner, email essentialdevteam@gmail.com with the installation ID shown in Settings › Privacy.

Backups

  • iPhone backup: your chats are included in your normal iCloud or computer backup of the phone, unless you turn off "Include chats in iPhone backup". Model files are never included.
  • iCloud Drive backup (optional, off by default): if you turn it on, the app writes a backup file to your own iCloud Drive, under your Apple ID. We have no access to it. You can encrypt manual backup files with a passphrase; we cannot recover a forgotten passphrase.
  • Export: chats can be exported as Markdown, JSON, or a backup file through the iOS share sheet, to a destination you choose.

Security

Chats are protected by iOS data protection and are inaccessible until the phone is unlocked after restart. You can require Face ID or your passcode to open the app, and the app blurs its contents in the app switcher. Deleting a chat or a model removes it from the app's storage; the app has no copy anywhere else.

Third-party AI models

The models you download are published by third parties (Google, Alibaba, DeepSeek, Meta, Microsoft, Mistral AI, Hugging Face) under their own licenses, shown in the app before download. They run entirely on your device. Their output is generated locally and is not reviewed by us or by the model publishers.

Children

The app is rated 17+ because the AI models are unfiltered and can produce content unsuitable for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Purchases

The app is bought once through the App Store, and the payment is handled by Apple. We receive no payment details or personal information from the transaction. If you bought QUIET Ai Pro (monthly or yearly) or Lifetime in an earlier version, that purchase is still checked on your device with Apple's StoreKit. Earlier subscriptions renew until you cancel them in your Apple Account settings.

This website

thequietai.app is hosted by GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses as any web server does. The domain's DNS is run by Cloudflare. The site uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising.

Your rights

Your chats and documents exist only on your device: delete them from Settings › Danger zone, or delete the app. If you turned on Share anonymous usage, you can ask us to access or delete that data by emailing essentialdevteam@gmail.com with your installation ID. Under GDPR, CCPA and similar laws you retain all your rights.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date. Any change to what the app collects comes with a new version of the app and an updated App Store privacy label.
